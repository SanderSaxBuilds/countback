Try the workflow

Open the workspace and choose Play the walkthrough for a scripted delivery with no API calls. Live modes need the judge access code. Play sample audio live streams four prerecorded clips through AssemblyAI without opening your microphone. You can also type counts, or choose Start receiving to use your microphone.

The sample clips count oat milk, coffee beans and paper cups: 72 oat milk bottles including 2 damaged, 24 coffee bags and 45 cup sleeves with a shortage of 5. The first oat milk count and its correction both stay in the history. The other three lines of the order can be counted by hand or by voice. Review the receipt, then export the CSV.

This is an English-language prototype with a fictional order and a three-minute voice session limit. Physical microphone testing, field trials, saved history and inventory integration are future work.
