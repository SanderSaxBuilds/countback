Try the workflow

Open the workspace and choose Try guided demo for a clearly labeled scripted example with no API calls. Live modes require the judge access code. Try live sample audio streams four prerecorded clips through AssemblyAI without opening your microphone. You can also type counts or choose Start receiving to use your microphone.

Expected sample result: 72 oat-milk bottles including 2 damaged, 24 coffee bags, and 45 cup sleeves with a shortage of 5. The first oat-milk count and its correction remain in history. Review the receipt, optionally edit a product, then export the CSV.

This is an English-language prototype with synthetic stock data, three fixed product lines, and a three-minute voice session limit. Physical microphone testing, field trials, persistence and inventory integration remain future work.
